The Local Chat Enquirer

New case

Case file local · fixed record

Who said it?

A person from the record is picked at random and one of their chat messages lands on the desk. Pick the name you think typed it. Get it wrong and another message and three clues turn up. Three wrong names and the suspect walks.

Every person is a guessing target once per run. Correct guesses close the file; a wrong third guess sends you back to the front page.

A local homage to whodunchat by DuckSaint — powered by a fixed set of people and messages in data.js.
